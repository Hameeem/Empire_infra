Highway Construction

  • Highway safety crash barriers

Structural Engineering

Bridge Construction

Bridge-related civil engineering and construction activities supporting transportation infrastructure.

Constructing vital river crossings, ravine bridges, and elevated viaducts. Scope includes substructure engineering (piers, abutments, pile caps), pre-stressed concrete (PSC) girders, deck slabs, bearings, and hydraulic scour protection.

  • Pre-stressed concrete (PSC) bridges
  • Deep pier & abutment foundations
  • Scour protection & guide bunds
  • Reinforced earth approach walls
