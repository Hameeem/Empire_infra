Company Profile

About Empire Construction and Infrastructure

Empire Construction and Infrastructure is a construction and infrastructure development company based in Jammu & Kashmir, India.

The company undertakes civil engineering and construction activities across roadways, highways, buildings, bridges and related infrastructure projects.

With a focus on professional execution and dependable construction solutions, the company aims to contribute to infrastructure development through quality-oriented work and responsible project management.

Operational Focus

  • Highways & Roadways: Regional corridors, bypasses, arterial networks, and hill road works.
  • Structures & Bridges: Concrete bridges, drainage structures, culverts, and civic buildings.
  • Heavy Machinery: Fleet mobilization including road rollers, motor graders, pavers, and earthmovers.
  • Location: Strategically operating across Jammu & Kashmir, India.

Our Objective

To participate in infrastructure development by delivering construction solutions that support connectivity, accessibility and the built environment.
