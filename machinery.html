Machinery & Equipment

Supporting construction and infrastructure activities through the use of road construction machinery and heavy equipment.

Fleet Showcase

Equipment. Capability. Execution.

Deploying heavy earthmoving, compaction, grading, and asphalt paving equipment to ensure structural compliance and timely execution.

Paving Fleet

Asphalt Road Pavers

High-precision mechanical sensor pavers utilized for uniform distribution, leveling, and pre-compaction of hot mix asphalt.

Purpose & Application Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM) and Bituminous Concrete (BC) highway surfacing.
Paving Width: 2.5m – 9.0m
Output Capacity: 300 – 600 TPH
Control Type: Sensor Electronic
Earthwork & Grading

Motor Graders

Articulated motor graders engineered for precision slope cutting, sub-grade profiling, aggregate spreading, and road camber shaping.

Purpose & Application Sub-base leveling, embankment shaping, and drainage ditch formation.
Moldboard Width: 12 – 14 Feet
Operating Weight: 14 – 17 Metric Tons
Configuration: Articulated 6x4 Drive
Compaction Fleet

Road Rollers

Heavy tandem vibratory road rollers engineered to deliver consistent compaction and superior surface density on asphalt overlays.

Purpose & Application Bituminous wearing courses, seal coats, and road surface smoothing.
Drum Configuration: Double Drum Tandem
Operating Weight: 9 – 12 Metric Tons
Vibration Frequency: 40 – 50 Hz
Sub-Base Compaction

Soil Compactors

High-amplitude single drum vibratory soil compactors (smooth and padfoot) for subgrade earth, granular sub-base, and embankment layers.

Purpose & Application Compacting clay, cohesive soils, gravel, and heavy embankment fills.
Drum Type: Smooth Drum / Padfoot
Operating Weight: 11 – 15 Metric Tons
Centrifugal Force: Up to 280 kN
Excavation & Earthwork

Excavators

Heavy-duty hydraulic crawler excavators equipped for mountain hill cutting, bulk earthmoving, foundation trenching, and rock loading.

Purpose & Application Bulk excavation, hill slope formation, retaining structure foundation digging.
Bucket Capacity: 0.9 – 1.4 m³
Operating Weight: 20 – 22 Metric Tons
Undercarriage: Heavy Steel Crawler
Bituminous Batching

Asphalt Mixing Plants

Stationary and modular asphalt batch mixing plants designed to produce high-specification hot mix asphalt for highway surfacing.

Purpose & Application Consistent batching of Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM) and asphalt concrete.
Rated Output: 80 – 160 TPH
Batch Control: Automated Computerized
Emission Control: Baghouse Filtration
Material Haulage

Tippers & Dump Trucks

Multi-axle heavy haulage tipper trucks for rapid transport of crushed aggregates, sub-base material, hot mix asphalt, and excavated earth.

Purpose & Application Logistics and material haulage across mountain road grades and work sites.
Payload Capacity: 16 – 25 Metric Tons
Axle Setup: 6x4 / 8x4 Heavy Multi-Axle
Body: Reinforced Rock / Tipper
Auxiliary Fleet

Heavy Construction Fleet

Auxiliary civil construction plant including wheel loaders, backhoe loaders, mobile concrete transit mixers, and diesel generators.

Purpose & Application Site material handling, structural concrete delivery, and remote site power.
Equipment Types: Wheel Loaders, Mixers, GenSets
Deployment: Project-Specific Mobilization
Terrain Rating: All-Terrain & High Altitude
